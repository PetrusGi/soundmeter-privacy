Privacy Policy — Sound Meter

Android application “Sound Meter” · Last updated 17 November 2026

Short version: the microphone audio is analysed in memory on your device and then thrown away. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or shared. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Audio

Sound Meter measures sound pressure level using your device's microphone. Audio is captured, converted to a sound level and a frequency spectrum in memory, and then discarded. No audio is recorded to storage, and no audio ever leaves your device. There is no upload, no cloud processing and no server involved in measurement.

2. Data stored on your device only

The app stores the following in its own private storage:

  • Session results: name, timestamp, duration, and the minimum, average, maximum, Leq (equivalent continuous level) and peak levels in decibels.
  • Your calibration offset and display preferences.

This data is never transmitted. You can delete individual sessions or all of them from inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything. The CSV export produces a file that only leaves the app when you choose where to send it using the Android share sheet.

3. Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. The app stores only a local flag indicating that the unlock was purchased, which it verifies with Google Play.

4. Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO — required to measure sound. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only by the Google Play Billing library to complete and restore a purchase.

No other permissions are requested.

5. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No account, login, email address or personal identifiers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files or location.

6. Children

Sound Meter is a general-purpose measurement tool and is not directed at children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent using the developer contact details published on the Sound Meter listing on Google Play.

Sound Meter is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any other sound measurement product or manufacturer.
